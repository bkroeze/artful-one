# Scorpion Paper Style Animation

Make an animation of a scorpion moving its appendages.

This animation will use the following set of images:
- body: `scorp_body.png`
- legs: `scorp_leg_1.png` through `scorp_leg_8.png`, starting numbering at the top right leg, and continuing clockwise
- claws: `scorp_claw_1.png` (right) and `scorp_claw_2.png` (left)
- tail: scorp_tail.png

# Animations

- body, does not move
- tail, swings left and right with the bottom of the image in an arc, pivoting on its pivot point
- legs 1 - 4: swing up and down with the right side of the image moving in an arc, pivoting on its pivot point
- legs 5 - 6: swing up and down with the left side of the image moving in an arc, pivoting on its pivot point

Canvas Size: 766,1080
