The patient spider is arranging the invisible.

A botanical-plate-inspired loading animation: one small spider keeps laying down silk, slowly making the web denser and denser while the bottom marquee mutters encouraging nonsense.

Script API: window.spiderLoader.start(), window.spiderLoader.stop(), and window.spiderLoader.reset(). The canvas defaults to 400×400, but redraws from its displayed size.
