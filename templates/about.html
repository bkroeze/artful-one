Leadership

I enjoy the people side of technical work: hiring, mentoring, explaining hard things, and helping teams make good decisions without getting buried in process.

Hiring

I’ve hired dozens of people, and I genuinely enjoy interviewing from either side of the table. A good interview should help both people figure out whether the fit is right.

Speaking

I’m comfortable in front of a room. I’ve spoken at DjangoCon and other technical conventions, and I enjoy making complex topics feel approachable.

Architecture

I specialize in making complex systems understandable, configurable, and repeatable. Good architecture is not just clever; it gives everyone involved a clearer way to reason about the work.

Finance and commerce

I understand accounting, cryptocurrency, multi-currency reconciliation, and the messy reality of moving money between systems.

I’ve worked with many vendor APIs and wrote some of the foundational backend e-commerce modules for Django. I’m comfortable in the space where software, money, reporting, and operational trust all overlap.

Art and creative collaboration

I do not generally do design work for clients, but I am an artist, and that changes how I approach creative projects.

I understand that a good site is not just a container for content. It needs to feel like the person, studio, project, or community behind it. I enjoy working with artists, site owners, and designers through an iterative process that turns a vision into something usable, expressive, and real.

Interests

Private, human-scale social apps

I’m interested in software that helps small groups connect, organize, remember, and collaborate without turning everything into a giant public feed.

The internet is very good at scale. I’m more interested in what happens when we build tools for trust, context, memory, and actual relationships.

Using AI effectively

I’m less interested in AI as a magic trick and more interested in AI as a practical extension of human work.

Things I’m exploring

  • Assistants with memory
  • Coding tools
  • Art tools
  • Creative workflows
  • Responsible automation
  • Small, useful agents

My Pixelborders app is one example of this: using AI and software to help with visual exploration while still leaving room for human taste and direction.

Digital privacy

I care about privacy, security, encryption, and clear disclosure.

That does not mean pretending every system can be perfectly private. It means being honest about what is collected, what is stored, what is shared, and what tradeoffs are being made.

Long-term growth

I care about making long-term growth possible: for myself, for the people I work with, and for the projects I help build.

That means choosing systems that can evolve, habits that compound, and collaborations where people can become more capable over time.
